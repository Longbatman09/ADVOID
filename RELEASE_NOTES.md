# ADVOID - Initial Release Notes (v1.0.0)

ADVOID is a modern, lightweight Android utility designed to enhance your music listening experience by automatically muting disruptive audio ads from your favorite streaming services.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎧 Seamless Ad Muting
ADVOID monitors notifications from selected music apps and instantly mutes your device's media volume when an ad is detected. Once the ad ends and your music returns, ADVOID restores your volume to its original level.

### 🔍 Smart Keyword Detection
Powered by a robust keyword engine, ADVOID identifies ads based on common patterns across multiple languages and platforms. 

### 📱 Extensive App Support (Whitelist)
Choose which apps ADVOID should monitor. Out-of-the-box support includes:
* Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music
* SoundCloud, Tidal, Deezer
* Amazon Music, Pandora, iHeartRadio
* JioSaavn, Gaana, Wynk, and more.

### 🔔 Notification Sound Customization
Know exactly when ADVOID is working. 
* **IN Sound:** Play a subtle chime when an ad is detected.
* **OUT Sound:** Play a sound when the ad ends and volume is restored.
* Use built-in sounds or upload your own custom audio files.

### 📊 Ad Detection Logs
Keep track of how much time you've saved. The logs screen shows which apps played ads, when they occurred, and how long they lasted.

### 🎨 Material 3 Design
A clean, intuitive interface inspired by Material Design 3.
* Full support for Light and Dark modes.
* Dynamic theme switching based on system settings.
* Landscape-optimized layouts.

### 🔒 Privacy First
* **Local Processing:** All notification scanning happens locally on your device.
* **No Data Collection:** ADVOID does not send any personal data or notification content off-device.
* **Open Disclosure:** Transparent setup process explaining exactly why each permission is needed.

## 🛠 Setup Requirements
To function correctly, ADVOID requires:
1. **Notification Access:** To "read" song titles and identify ads.
2. **Battery Optimization Bypass:** To ensure the service isn't killed in the background.
3. **Post Notifications (Android 13+):** To show the active status in your drawer.
