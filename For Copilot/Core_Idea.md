Core Idea,
Detects when selected apps start playing media via notification access
Detects the notification contains “Advertisement” ,”Ad” or other word related to advertisement.
Automatically mutes the media volume when an notification with “Advertisement” in it pops out
Restores volume when the notification of the recorded app vanishes (basically when the advertisement overs) , this can be detected by the same app notification and when it did not contain the word “advertisement”
